Which pattern does this match?

"Price hike" covers several genuinely different mechanisms — a real across-the-board pricing change, your own promo period simply ending, or not a price change at all but a new line item you didn't notice getting added. Click through to figure out what to verify and where.

Base price raised outright

A platform-wide pricing change, rarely announced by email — you have to compare your billing history against the current public pricing page yourself.

Promo code or discount expired

The discounted renewal price was never permanent — it was tied to a fixed number of cycles and reverts to list price automatically, with an identical-looking invoice.

A paid add-on got bundled in

Not a base-price increase — a pre-checked add-on or plugin fee got added to the renewal, so the total is bigger but the unit price didn't move.

Credits expired, forcing an upgrade

Usage credits reset to zero and you're pushed into a pricier tier just to have enough headroom — a price increase in everything but name.
