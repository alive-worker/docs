Virtual Cards for AI Subscriptions: A Complete Guide

A virtual card is the most common way to pay for overseas AI subscriptions, but opening one involves more decisions than it looks like: which card type, how strict the provider's vetting is, what limit lets a charge succeed without over-exposing your funds, and whether multiple subscriptions should share one card or use separate ones. These 10 articles start with the overall strategy, then work down to specific limit, expiry, and allocation decisions, ending with a troubleshooting guide for declined payments. Set the expiry too short and renewals fail; set it too long and the card becomes a bigger risk if compromised — trade-offs like this run through every article in this group.
