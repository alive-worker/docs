AI Subscription Billing & Account Management

Billing and account issues are the category most likely to get overlooked until they become a real problem — a removed team seat that doesn't lower the invoice, a free tier's usage cap dropping overnight, a trial that silently turns into an annual plan, a startup shutting down before you can get a refund. These 28 articles cover nearly every anomaly scenario an overseas AI subscription can produce, organized into four groups: billing anomalies and hidden charges, price hikes and chargeback disputes, account security emergencies, and team or family subscription management. Several articles give literal step-by-step checklists — like the three steps you shouldn't skip before disputing a charge — rather than general advice.
